Level Waves placeholder sounds: sources and licences

Every clip in this folder is an edit of a recording released under CC0 1.0 (public-domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 allows copying, editing and redistribution, including commercially and in a public repository, with no attribution required. The authors are credited here anyway. Nothing was bought, and nothing is taken from commercial games, films or videos.

Downloaded 2026-11-01 (first set) and 2026-11-02 (audio pass 2). Freesound sounds were taken as Freesound's high-quality Ogg previews (the originals need a login); each one's licence was checked on its Freesound page. Edits for Solar Divers: trimming at the measured onset, filtering/EQ, layering, level flattening (rocket motor loop), fades, speed changes of at most 3 % between variants, peak normalisation to -1 dBFS, conversion to 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit WAV. The edit recipe is described in Level-Design-Waves-Boss.md (Asset credits).
