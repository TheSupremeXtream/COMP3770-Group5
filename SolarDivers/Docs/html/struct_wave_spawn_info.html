Detailed Description

What WaveEncounter knows about a spawned enemy's place in its wave. Passed once, right after spawning, to any component on the enemy that implements IWaveSpawnReceiver. Optional: enemies without one are unaffected. Offsets are in the spawn origin's local space (+X = origin's right).
