Detailed Description

PLACEHOLDER homing rocket for the test enemies (not Taha's weapons or Leonardo's AI). Dodgeable by design: it flies straight for Arming Seconds, then turns towards the player at no more than Turn Rate while it speeds up to Max Speed, stops homing after Homing Seconds (burn-out: flies straight) and is removed after Lifetime or when it leaves its area. A sharp sideways move inside its turning circle makes it miss. It can be shot down (TestEnemyHealth on the same object) and hurts on contact (TestContactDamage). Moves in its parent's space, so it follows a moving rig. Needs a trigger collider and a kinematic Rigidbody.
