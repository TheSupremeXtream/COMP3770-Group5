PLACEHOLDER homing rocket for the test enemies (not Taha\textquotesingle{}s weapons or Leonardo\textquotesingle{}s AI). Dodgeable by design: it flies straight for Arming Seconds, then turns towards the player at no more than Turn Rate while it speeds up to Max Speed, stops homing after Homing Seconds (burn-\/out: flies straight) and is removed after Lifetime or when it leaves its area. A sharp sideways move inside its turning circle makes it miss. It can be shot down (\doxylink{class_test_enemy_health}{Test\+Enemy\+Health} on the same object) and hurts on contact (\doxylink{class_test_contact_damage}{Test\+Contact\+Damage}). Moves in its parent\textquotesingle{}s space, so it follows a moving rig. Needs a trigger collider and a kinematic Rigidbody.
