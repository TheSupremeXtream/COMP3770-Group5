\hypertarget{class_wave_encounter}{}\label{class_wave_encounter}\index{WaveEncounter@{WaveEncounter}}


Runs a list of enemy waves in order. Spawning only: enemy movement/\+\+AI lives on the enemy prefabs. An enemy stops counting toward its wave when it is destroyed/\+disabled, leaves the encounter bounds, or is force-\/removed by the clear timeout. "{}\+Cleared"{} means every wave finished; it does not mean every enemy was destroyed (see All\+Enemies\+Destroyed). \doxylink{class_wave_encounter_a8dd9180d1efdca5f120c2980328a9278}{Fail\+Encounter()} ends it without victory.
