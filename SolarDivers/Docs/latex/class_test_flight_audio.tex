PLACEHOLDER engine sound for the wave test scene (cinematic, not a flight-\/physics simulation). Player engine: one recorded jet loop whose level, pitch and brightness follow the ship\textquotesingle{}s speed and acceleration smoothly (spools up faster than it winds down), fading out on defeat. Enemy flybys: when a test enemy is about to pass close to the player, a recorded jet pass is started so its loudest point lines up with the closest approach. The voice then follows the enemy: level and brightness from distance, stereo position from its side, and a small, capped Doppler-\/style pitch change from the closing speed. Restart and leaving Play mode stop every loop.
