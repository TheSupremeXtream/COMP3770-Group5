Every clip in this folder is an {\bfseries{edit of a recording released under CC0 1.\+0}} (public-\/domain dedication, \href{https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/}{\texttt{https:\+/\+/\+creativecommons.\+org/\+publicdomain/\+zero/\+1.\+0/}}). CC0 allows copying, editing and redistribution, including commercially and in a public repository, with no attribution required. The authors are credited here anyway. Nothing was bought, and nothing is taken from commercial games, films or videos.

Downloaded 2026-\/11-\/01 (first set) and 2026-\/11-\/02 (audio pass 2). Freesound sounds were taken as Freesound\textquotesingle{}s high-\/quality Ogg previews (the originals need a login); each one\textquotesingle{}s licence was checked on its Freesound page. Edits for Solar Divers: trimming at the measured onset, filtering/\+\+EQ, layering, level flattening (rocket motor loop), fades, speed changes of at most 3 \% between variants, peak normalisation to -\/1 d\+BFS, conversion to 44.\+1 k\+Hz mono 16-\/bit WAV. The edit recipe is described in {\ttfamily Level-\/\+Design-\/\+Waves-\/\+Boss.\+md} (Asset credits).
