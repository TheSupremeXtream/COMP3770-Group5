# Level Waves placeholder sounds: sources and licences

Every clip in this folder is an **edit of a recording released under CC0 1.0** (public-domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 allows copying, editing and redistribution, including
commercially and in a public repository, with no attribution required. The authors are credited here anyway.
Nothing was bought, and nothing is taken from commercial games, films or videos.

Downloaded 2026-11-01 (first set) and 2026-11-02 (audio pass 2). Freesound sounds were taken as Freesound's
high-quality Ogg previews (the originals need a login); each one's licence was checked on its Freesound page.
Edits for Solar Divers: trimming at the measured onset, filtering/EQ, layering, level flattening (rocket motor loop),
fades, speed changes of at most 3 % between variants, peak normalisation to -1 dBFS, conversion to 44.1 kHz mono
16-bit WAV. The edit recipe is described in `Level-Design-Waves-Boss.md` (Asset credits).

built from other packs. A CC0-labelled upload of a commercial effects library on the Internet Archive was not used,
because the uploader's licence could not be verified.
