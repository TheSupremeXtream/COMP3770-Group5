# Level Waves placeholder sounds: sources and licences

Every clip in this folder is an **edit of a recording released under CC0 1.0** (public-domain dedication,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). CC0 allows copying, editing and redistribution, including
commercially, with no attribution required. The authors are credited here anyway. Nothing was bought.

Downloaded 2026-11-01. Edits (trimming, filtering, layering, speed changes of at most 0.55-1.15x, fades, peak
normalisation to -1 dBFS, conversion to 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit WAV) were made for Solar Divers. The edit recipe is
listed in `Level-Design-Waves-Boss.md` (Asset credits).
