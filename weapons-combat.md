# Taha Hammoud - Weapons and Combat ( My milestone 1 Contribution)

Weapons and Combat

I worked on the weapons and combat section of the GDD.

In sum, the player uses 3 different weapons to destroy enemies and obstacles while progressing through the levels. Different weapons have different damage, fire rate, projectile speed, ammo capacity, and cooldown.


Proposed weapon values:

| Weapon | Damage | Fire Rate | Projectile Speed | Ammo | Cooldown |

| Laser Cannon | 10 | 5 shots/sec | Fast | Unlimited | 0 sec |
| Plasma Beams | 27 | 2 shots/sec | Medium-fast | 33 | 0.5 sec |
| Missile Launcher | 65 | 0.5 shots/sec | Slow | 6 missiles | 2 sec |
